Spotify Wrap

Every year, we wonder what our top artist, song, or genre is. Sometimes when we see it, we don't believe it because we're like, “Oh, this was a song from the very beginning of the year, compared to what I normally listen to.” Here, I wanted to recreate that by letting you break it down by month and find similar songs that fit that vibe.
